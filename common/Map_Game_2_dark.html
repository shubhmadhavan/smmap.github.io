    Keyboard shortcuts

    Click a placeShow or hide its name. Alt + click keeps it painted.
    RReveal or hide every name
    CShow names as cards with leader lines, or as labels
    Shift CSwitch card lines: straight, elbow, curve
    Drag a label or cardMove it (cards remember where you put them). Double-click to select its text.
    T Ctrl IShow the notes inside the labels
    F4Open or close the list
    /Filter the list
    ↑ ↓ EnterMove through the list and highlight on the map
    W A S DPan (Shift + A / D is faster)
    F GZoom in / out (also Shift + W / S)
    ZChange label size
    I J K LNudge the label you last clicked
    Ctrl 1–4Grey, green, beige or standard map
    BSwitch between the India image and the tile map (with themes)
    Ctrl HAll maps
    EscClose this, or leave the full-screen list
